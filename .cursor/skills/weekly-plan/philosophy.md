# Weekly Plan Philosophy

Derived from 30+ Notion pages (Jun 2024 – Jun 2026). This is the *why* behind the format — what the weekly plan is for, not just how to fill it in.

---

## What this system is

The weekly plan is a **focus dashboard**: a single page you open during the week to remember what you decided matters, why it matters, and whether you're on track. It is not a task list, not a journal, and not a project tracker. Those live elsewhere (Linear, inbox, daily review). The weekly plan answers one question:

> **"Am I working on the right things this week — the things that ladder to what I care about over the next six months?"**

When you followed this religiously, the pattern was consistent: retro → narrow to 3–8 intentional goals with stated purpose → grade honestly → carry forward or defer the rest → link back to longer horizons.

When you stopped, the pages still existed but retros got skipped, goal counts ballooned (10+ items), and the 6-month callout became decorative.

---

## The three horizons

| Horizon | Where | Role in the system |
|---------|-------|-------------------|
| **6-month** | Goal Tracker DB | *Direction* — what kind of person/leader you're becoming |
| **Weekly** | Personal + Professional plan pages | *Commitment* — what you'll actually do this week |
| **Daily** | Calendar, daily review, morning brief | *Execution* — how the day maps to the week |

The weekly plan is the **bridge** between aspiration (6-month) and action (daily). Without it, daily work becomes reactive. With it, you can look at any task and ask "does this serve #3 this week?"

The callout that appeared on early pages and persists today states this explicitly:

> *"This should be the 2-3 most important things I should be working on this week to work towards my 6 month goals"*

The agent should treat **2–3 as the target, 3–8 as the ceiling**. More than 8 means the week isn't focused — push items to Deferred.

## Core rituals (stable since Oct 2024)

### 1. Retro before plan
Grade last week before setting this week. Skipping retro (Nov 2025, Oct 2024 gaps) correlates with unfocused weeks. The grading symbols create accountability without shame:
- ✅ Complete — move on
- 🆗 80%+ — good enough; note what's left
- 🛑 Deferred — explicit choice, not silent failure
- ❌ Not complete — learn from it

### 2. Purpose on every goal
Even one line. The Purpose block is what turns a task into an intentional commitment. Early pages (Jun 2024) used "Goal" instead of "Purpose" — same intent. The agent must always ask "why does this matter this week?"

### 3. "3 ways I could have done better"
Recurring themes across 18 months:
- Too much TV / low stress reducing drive
- Not timeboxing / not protecting calendar blocks
- Research expanding instead of shipping (writing goals especially)

These improvement cues are **meta-habits** — they recur because they're the actual levers, not the surface tasks.

### 4. Deferred is a parking lot, not a graveyard
Items live in Deferred with optional Reflection when they're genuinely paused. The same deferred items appear for months (writing theory, Docier updates, Tone POC) — that's fine. They're visible, not forgotten.

### 5. One Pager — the focus anchor
Title evolved: "Building consensus with different voices" (Jun 2024) → "What helps me focus" (Aug 2024 onward). Core Ideas section tracks flow-state research. This is the **philosophical north star** for the week — optional but powerful when filled.

### 6. Primary Focus (DB property)
One thematic lens per week: Personal Growth → Business Growth → Time Management → PV System, etc. Sets the tone before you read individual goals.

## Personal vs Professional — different modes

| | Personal | Professional |
|---|----------|--------------|
| **Mindset** | Life design, habits, learning, relationships, founder journey | Team alignment, delivery, risk tracking, stakeholder comms |
| **Goal shape** | `#N:` H2 blocks with Purpose/Sub-tasks | Numbered list under Xavier with Purpose/Subtasks |
| **Audience** | Yourself | Yourself + team (Released checkbox) |
| **Extra sections** | One Pager, improvement cues | Milestones, Highlights, Sharks/Shipwrecks |
| **Typical count** | 3–8 goals | 4–6 goals |
| **When** | Sunday AM, or life-planning moments | Sunday PM / Monday AM, before team sync |

They share bootstrap (6-month goals, OWNER_GOALS.md) but should be **separate sessions**. Mixing them dilutes focus — your basic-agent already ran Personal first, then Professional.

## What "focused intently" means in practice

From your best weeks (Nov 2024, Apr 2025, Aug 2025 when Pass):

1. **Few goals, sharp purpose** — "Release CO2 article" not "work on writing"
2. **Sub-tasks are checkable** — you graded individual sub-tasks, not just the parent
3. **6-month connection is implicit or explicit** — reading → learning goal; Substack → founder brand; PV article → technical credibility
4. **Deferred stays deferred** — you didn't let parking-lot items leak into the active 8
5. **Retro informs next week** — failed reading goal → carried forward with same structure; deferred writing → Reflection captured why

The agent's job is to **enforce focus**, not just transcribe wishes. When the user proposes 12 goals, push back: "Which 3 are the 6-month bridge this week?"

---

## Anti-patterns (observed when discipline slipped)

- Retro section empty or skipped entirely
- "This Week's Goal Setting" populated without grading last week
- 10+ goals with duplicate numbering (#6 twice)
- Purpose left as "xxx" or copied from another goal
- No connection to any 6-month goal for the entire week
- Professional plan never marked Released
- Same improvement cues copy-pasted without new reflection

---

## Agent principles

1. **Dashboard, not dump** — every goal earns its place by tying to purpose and (ideally) a 6-month goal
2. **Interview, don't assume** — goals are co-created in conversation, never auto-populated
3. **Honest grading** — retro is a learning loop, not a report card
4. **Preserve format fidelity** — templates exist so the page is scannable mid-week
5. **Separate contexts** — personal life design ≠ professional delivery; route accordingly
